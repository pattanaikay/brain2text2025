Speech leaves before the brain does.

ALS and brainstem stroke take the muscles, not the message. Patients hold the sentence clearly in cortex and can’t push it through the throat. A speech BCI listens to the cortical signal directly and types or speaks the sentence back.

The field’s threshold for usefulness is word-error rate. Below ~5 % a decoder reads as fluent; above ~15 % it reads as broken. The gap between those numbers is where everything important happens.

The bet, in one breath

Two decoders.
One harness.
Seven branches.

Take Zhang’s BIT_Transformer baseline as the trunk. Replace only the neural encoder, keep the projector, the quantized Qwen LLM, and every training script intact. Sweep seven alternatives through the same pipeline and on the same data — and let the harness surface the two decoders that matter: an accuracy front-runner, and a quantized cousin that runs on a consumer laptop.

02 — MAP
06 / 10

Scale: more parameters, smarter parameters.

B · 01
Conformer
iPhoneme · ConformerXL

Twelve macaron blocks pair attention with depthwise convolution. The conv adds a local inductive bias that stabilises the early training trajectory; the cost is 4× the parameters of the BIT baseline.

Risk: the macaron FFN is heavy; we’ll need higher LR warmup and longer training to avoid underfitting.

B · 06
MoE
EEGMoE · NeuroMoE

Replace the FFN with six specific experts (top-2 routed) plus two shared experts. Five times the FFN capacity, only roughly twice the active compute — load-balance loss keeps the routing honest.

Risk: expert collapse if the auxiliary load-balance weight is set wrong; tune --aux_loss_weight early.

02 — MAP
07 / 10

Linear-time: pay once per timestep.

B · 02
Mamba / POSSM
POSSM hybrid SSM

Cross-attention spike tokenizer feeds seven Mamba blocks; cost is O(T·d²) instead of O(T²·d). On the toy stream Mamba was already the fastest encoder — that lead widens as the BCI stream lengthens.

Risk: on CPU we use a GRU fallback; on GPU mamba-ssm needs careful d_state init and tight dt-bias bounds.

B · 07
HRM
Hierarchical Reasoning Model

Two recurrences at two clocks — L at 20 ms, H at 100 ms — with a fixed-point DEQ solver and a one-step gradient. The result is O(1) memory in sequence length and the smallest parameter count in the lineup at 2.9 M.

Risk: deeply sequential, so the GPU will accelerate it less than the parallel attentions; budget extra epochs.

02 — MAP
08 / 10

Adapt, regularize, and swap the head.

B · 04
ZenBrain
7-layer dual memory

Self-attention is paired with a cross-attention over an episodic FIFO buffer. At training time the buffer is empty; at inference it fills with the user’s own recent trials so the decoder adapts on the fly — zero-calibration.

B · 03
TopoLoss
TopoNets · arXiv 2024

A drop-in loss term that pulls the encoder’s feature map toward a cortically plausible topography. Costs nothing at inference, ablates cleanly via --topo_weight τ.

B · 05
Audio-LLM swap
Qwen2-Audio · Whisper · Phi-4-MM

Keep the BIT encoder; swap the language head. A model that’s already heard speech may decode neural data closer to speech with less projector training.

03 — BEHAVIOUR
09 / 10

What the toy stream already says.

B = 4, T = 200 bins (~4 s), 512 channels. Latency is a numpy-simulated forward pass on CPU; FLOPs extrapolate to T = 800 bins (16 s) — the long end of real BCI streams. Rows are sorted by parameter count.

Read across each row. Mamba sits in the middle on capacity but at the floor on latency — its scan vectorises beautifully. HRM is the opposite — smallest model, largest latency, because its dual-clock recurrence is sequential and refuses to fuse. On GPU, that gap shrinks for the parallel encoders and shrinks only slightly for the sequential ones; the FLOPs column is the honest GPU predictor.

04 — BETS
10 / 10

Two decoders. Two finish lines.

Bet 01 · Accuracy
Match Zhang on the test split.

Pick the best WER from the seven branches, train to convergence at full scale, and beat or match the 4.9 % validation WER on the held-out competition test set with the same data and harness.

Bet 02 · Reach
Fit it on a laptop.

Pick the lightest branch that stays within 1.5 WER of bet 1, quantize the LLM to 4-bit NF4 and the encoder to int8, and demonstrate streaming inference on a 6 GB consumer GPU or Apple silicon.
